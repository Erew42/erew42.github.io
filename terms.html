Terms of use

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Intended use

pmkt is a personal research archive utility operated by Erew42. It is intended to store and verify research recordings selected by the operator in the operator’s own Google Drive account.

Account authorization

Use the utility only with accounts and files you are authorized to access. Google Drive access requires the account owner’s consent and remains subject to Google’s applicable service terms. The account owner can revoke access through Google Account connections.

Operating the archive

The operator controls which recordings are uploaded, which local copies are retained and when archive files are removed. Uploads and restoration depend on the local computer, network connection, Google authorization and Google Drive availability. A successful upload is not, by itself, a substitute for an integrity and restoration check.

Data handling

The privacy notice explains what information the configured workflow accesses, how it is used and stored, and how access can be revoked.

Questions

Contact the operator through the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
