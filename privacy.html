Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This notice describes the Google Drive access used by pmkt, a personal research archive utility operated by Erew42.

Information accessed

  • Research archives and folders created by or explicitly authorized for the app in the consenting account’s Google Drive.
  • Associated file names, identifiers, sizes, timestamps and checksums needed to store, locate and verify those archives.
  • Drive storage quota information used to check whether uploads have sufficient space.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google to authorize the configured uploader.

The uploader requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permission is limited to app-created or explicitly authorized files; it does not grant general access to all existing Drive files.

Purpose and use

Information is used to upload completed research archives, check their integrity, download copies for restoration checks and manage the operator’s research storage. The uploader does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or generalized artificial-intelligence model training.

Storage and access

Archive files are stored in the operator’s Google Drive. Local source files, staging archives, restoration copies and verification receipts may also be retained on the operator’s computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally outside source repositories with access restricted through filesystem permissions.

Google processes Drive data under its own terms and privacy policies. The configured workflow transfers archive data between the operator’s computer and Google Drive. This public information website contains no archive contents or OAuth credentials.

Retention and deletion

Archives and local copies remain until the operator deletes them. The operator can delete app-created Drive files through Google Drive and remove local copies and credentials from their own computer. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not itself delete previously stored files.

Your controls

The account owner can review or revoke the app’s access in Google Account connections. Questions and requests about this personal utility can be directed to the support email displayed on its Google consent screen.

This information website

The website contains no advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard website request information under the GitHub privacy statement. Google sign-in and Google Drive operate under Google’s privacy policy.

Google API data use

pmkt’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the configured utility’s data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
